# Disagreement classification

Primary and secondary readings are preserved exactly. Disagreements are classified as character-level, word-boundary, abbreviation-expansion, line-order, illegible-text, region-boundary, punctuation/editorial-mark, confidence, or omitted/added-text differences.

The comparison layer never silently selects a preferred reading. Substantive differences remain open or are promoted to uncertainty. Cosmetic differences may still be recorded because they can reveal divergent editorial policies.
