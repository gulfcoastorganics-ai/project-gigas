# Line and region review conventions

Line-review records retain normalized image coordinates, region and line IDs, proposed diplomatic reading when supplied, illegible or damaged markers, abbreviations, confidence, comments, evidence references, submission hash, reviewer identity, and timestamp.

Coordinates must remain between 0 and 1 with positive dimensions. Reviewers should assess boundaries, columns, reading order, rubrication, decorated initials, marginal material, damage, and omissions. Synthetic records may exercise validation, but must remain outside canonical and scholarly submission data.
