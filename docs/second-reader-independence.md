# Second-reader independence

The second reader receives manuscript evidence, folio identity, applicable policy, and safe provenance metadata, but not the primary wording, confidence, commentary, comparison output, or adjudication notes. The second reader must declare independent reading, no prior access to the primary reading, and conflicts or prior collaboration.

Independence is a documented workflow condition, not a claim that reviewers have never met or worked in the same field. Any disclosed collaboration is retained and can block or require project review. The same contributor cannot satisfy both required readings.
