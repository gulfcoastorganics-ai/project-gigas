# Source rights and provenance

Rights are recorded separately from manuscript age. The Codex Gigas is medieval, but that fact alone does not establish copyright or license status for a modern digitized image. Use an institutional rights statement, public-domain mark, or explicit license basis.

Allowed candidate-transcription states are `public_domain`, `public_domain_mark`, `cc0`, `cc_by`, `cc_by_sa`, and `institution_permission`. `unknown` and `restricted` are blocked by default. The rights record also preserves attribution requirements and the distinction between the underlying work and the digital image.

Verify a receipt with:

```sh
npm run verify:source-receipt -- --path=data/candidates/external-sources/<source-id>/source-receipt.json
```
