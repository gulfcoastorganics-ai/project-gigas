# Folio 2r textual source strategy

## Sources examined

- National Library of Sweden / manuscripta.se A 148 catalog record
- National Library of Sweden A 148 IIIF manifest and image service

## Selected basis

No transcription source is selected. The examined records establish folio identity, sequence, image provenance, repository, and license metadata, but do not provide a reviewed diplomatic transcription for folio 2r.

The canonical diplomatic collection therefore remains empty and explicitly labeled `human-project-draft`. No Latin or English has been reconstructed from a modern Bible, OCR, AI output, or memory.

## Editorial policy for future entry

- Orthography: preserve visible manuscript spelling.
- Abbreviations: preserve marks in diplomatic text; expand only in the separate editorial layer.
- Line breaks: enter only after line regions are authored.
- Punctuation: do not modernize silently.
- Rubrication: record structurally.
- Damage: mark damaged or illegible spans; never reconstruct.
- Uncertainty: use structured records with confidence and reasons.
- Machine assistance: none used for the canonical pilot text.

External review by a qualified Latin/manuscript specialist is required before any text layer can move beyond draft.
